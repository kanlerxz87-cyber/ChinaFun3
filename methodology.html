Verification Protocol

Zero Hallucinations: How Every Value Is Checked.

Four strict rules govern every cell on the ChinaFun3 root dashboard.

1. Primary Official Sources First

Dates come from gov.cn; visa rules from en.nia.gov.cn; restaurant stars, price bands, and addresses from guide.michelin.com; hotel rates from live Feb 15–22, 2027 checkout queries and brand sites; menus from official 2026 PDFs/FAQs; attractions from yugarden.com.cn, shanghaimuseum.net, shanghaidisneyresort.com, and english.shanghai.gov.cn.

2. Blank + Flagged Over Guessing

When an official portal fails (e.g., shmetro.com, orientalpearltower.com.cn) or a venue withholds 2027 pricing (Disneyland Feb 2027, unpublished restaurant tasting menus), the cell stays blank and receives an explicit amber flag.

3. Exact Arithmetic Labels

Two-adult totals are printed only when exact per-person or for-two official figures exist (e.g., Taian Table ¥2,588×2 = ¥5,176; Sir Elly's ¥1,288×2×1.166 = ¥3,003.62; Yu Garden ¥30×2 = ¥60). MICHELIN ¥–¥¥¥¥ tiers are never converted into guessed currency totals.

4. Live Re-Checkable URLs

Hotel rows link directly to the parameterized arr=2027-02-15&dep=2027-02-22&nA=2&nC=0&nR=1 MICHELIN Guide Hotels query so you can verify live inventory and checkout currency anytime.
