Why this ranking? (proof you can re-do in 2 minutes)

Method: From hotel’s nearest Metro station, we used Shanghai Metro official journey planner (shmetro.com) + Amap (Gaode) transit time at 11:00 weekday off-peak. “Within 1h” means door-to-door: hotel walk (6–8 min) + metro + 5-min exit walk ≤60 min.

  • Huangpu hotels: East Nanjing Rd L2/10 reaches Jing’an Temple (Fu1039 area) 18 min, Xintiandi (Fu He Hui) 14 min, Lujiazui (Yong Fu) 12 min via L2.
  • Pudong hotels: Lujiazui→East Nanjing Rd 12 min (L2 one stop) → then as above; so 38 vs. 44 is the fringe (far Changning/Pudong Bib venues tipping over 60 min at peak).
  • All counts use the same 2024 Michelin Guide Shanghai starred list (51 starred city-wide). Count table in Sources → Michelin.
Translation: Any hotel ranked #1–4 gives you every restaurant on the Michelin shortlist within 1h. Pudong (#5–6) still reaches all 14 shortlist venues within 1h; you only lose a few fringe Bib spots outside the shortlist (not needed for a week’s dinners).
