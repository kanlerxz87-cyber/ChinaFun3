How the ranking is built

Score

score = 10 × (starred restaurants in the same building) + (starred restaurants whose district is confirmed on MICHELIN)

The building term dominates because it is exact: a restaurant on another floor of your hotel is a two-minute walk, unambiguously inside the one-hour rule. The district term is a verified floor, not a total.

What is not claimed

No door-to-door travel time is published here. MICHELIN pages give the district, not a transit time, and a journey-time figure without a routing source would be a guess. Per-restaurant minutes need a routing check — logged on Open Questions.
