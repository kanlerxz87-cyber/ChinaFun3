Choose the right branch

PEOPLE’S SQUARE

Not the permanent collection

During the trip window, this branch is dedicated to the Americas exhibition. Do not plan a permanent-collection visit here. Evening sessions are not guaranteed.

EAST

For permanent displays

The official People’s Square guide directs visitors seeking permanent displays to East. Its Tuesday closure differs from People’s Square’s Monday closure. Ticketed exhibitions have separate rules.
