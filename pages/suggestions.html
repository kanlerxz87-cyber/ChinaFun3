Questions that must be answered before any decision

No decision (hotel, dates, restaurants) is reasonable until Q1–Q6 are answered from official sources. Each question lists how to discover the missing information.

#QuestionWhy it blocks the decisionHow to discover it
Q1What is your passport nationality? Do you need a visa for Feb 2027?Visa-free expires 31 Dec 2026; wrong assumption = denied entry.Check NIA list + embassy site for your passport; apply for L visa if required.
Q2Which 7-night option (A–H) do you prefer, and is it bookable?Price and availability differ by date.Open official hotel booking for each option; record totals.
Q3Which hotel area do you prefer: Bund, Lujiazui, or Jing'an?Determines 1h Michelin access and nightly total.Compare verified 7-night totals + Metro times from each area.
Q4Which Michelin restaurants do you want (starred vs Bib Gourmand)?Starred tables need advance booking; closed days vary.Shortlist from Restaurants page; check each official page for hours/booking.
Q5What is your per-person restaurant budget for 2 adults?¥¥¥¥ tasting menus can exceed a hotel night; couple total must be budgeted.Read official menus; record exact per-person price ×2.
Q6Do you want 7 nights or 14 nights (second city)?Changes hotel total and itinerary.Decide after Q2–Q5; 14-night = 2×7-night totals + intercity transport (verify separately).
Q7Are there dietary restrictions, allergies, or mobility needs?Affects restaurant and activity choices.Note in data/ and filter tables accordingly.
Q8What is your total trip budget (hotels + food + activities + transport)?Without it, “cheapest” has no meaning.Set a cap; compare verified totals against it.
