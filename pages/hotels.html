Why Bund first? The 2026 guide places multiple two-star restaurants in Huangpu (Canton 8, Imperial Treasure Huangpu, Cheng Long Hang area) and the Bund hotels sit on Line 2 with direct access to People's Square, East Nanjing Road, and Lujiazui. Lujiazui hotels are equally strong for Pudong one-star restaurants. Jing'an is central but slightly less dense for starred dining. All five areas are within ~1h of the Shanghai Museum, Yu Garden, and Oriental Pearl Tower by Metro (verify exact station times on shmetro.com / Metro app).

How to get the real 7-night total (no guessing)

  1. Pick a date option from the Itinerary page (e.g., 15–22 Feb 2027).
  2. Open the official booking link in the row above (Hilton / Peninsula / Hyatt / Shangri-La / Puli) — not a third-party reseller.
  3. Select 2 adults, 1 room, queen/king/double-queen, enter exact check-in/check-out, and read the total for 7 nights including taxes/fees.
  4. Record the total + currency + cancellation terms in data/hotels.csv and mark price_verified=yes only with a screenshot/link.
  5. Repeat for all 5 hotels and compare totals. Cheapest = lowest verified 7-night total for the same dates and room type.

Best time to book: Feb 2027 is ~3 months from research (Nov 2026). Rates should be live on official sites now — book the verified cheapest refundable rate, then re-check weekly for drops. Do not wait for a “deal” without live data. If a date shows “not yet available,” that date is not bookable — choose the next available 7-night window.
