Why 15–22 February works

Your preferred window opens two days after the nine-day Spring Festival holiday ends, which removes the two biggest risks of a February China trip: national-holiday pricing and holiday closures at temples, museums and restaurants. It also sits early enough to avoid March's heavier rain (roughly 86–88 mm vs 65 mm in February). The Lantern Festival on 20 February falls inside the stay and is the one evening worth planning around.

Recommended approach for these dates

  1. Now → ~6 months out: book a free-cancellation rate at the first-choice hotel as soon as the February 2027 calendar opens. This buys availability of the room type you need — a queen-or-larger bed with an ensuite bath — without locking the price.
  2. Inside 8–14 days: re-check the live rate. The 2026 industry data points to this window as the cheapest for city hotels; with a cancellable booking in hand, a lower rate can simply replace it.
  3. Never rely on last-minute pricing around 5–13 February — that is the holiday peak this itinerary deliberately avoids.

Caveat worth stating plainly: the studies measure average behaviour across many city hotels. A specific luxury property with a specific room category may never discount, and waiting can cost the room rather than save money.

If you stretch to the full two weeks

The trip window is 15 Feb – 23 Mar 2027, so 7 nights is the short option and 14 is the long one. Nothing in this plan breaks if you extend — the only real change is that day trips become worth the travel time.
