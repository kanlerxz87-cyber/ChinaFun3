What "verified" means here — and what it does not

It means

  • The value appears on the linked official page.
  • The link resolves to the brand, restaurant or guide itself.
  • Ambiguity is written down rather than smoothed over (see 2★ count, price tiers).

It does not mean

  • It will still be true in February 2027 — hours and prices change.
  • It is a booking quote. Prices require the live engine.
  • It is complete. Gaps are counted on Flagged.
