Executive summary • Decision in 3 minutes

Shanghai is the strongest “hotel + Michelin within 1 hour” bet for Feb–Mar 2027.

Your 1–2 week window, 7-night model, 2 adults, queen+ & private bath, and “≤1 hour by subway/bus” constraint all point to Huangpu on the Bund as base, with Pudong Lujiazui as skyline-view backup. This page distills the 4 decisions you need to make — with source-linked proof elsewhere.

Scope: Feb 15–Mar 23, 2027 is 37 days. We model 7-night stays (the shortest “week” + the cheapest way hotels show totals). Preferred Feb 15–18, 2027 pinned to Option A below. Chinese New Year 2027 is Feb 6 — so Feb 15 is post-CNY travel peak, some kitchens still on holiday rotas (flagged).

Decision 1 — Where to base?

Recommendation: Huangpu — The Bund (The Peninsula Shanghai or Waldorf Astoria on the Bund).

  • Why: Highest Michelin density within 1h. Huangpu/Jing’an/Xuhui clusters (Canton 8, Lao Zheng Xing, Jin Xuan, Yong Fu, Fu He Hui, 8½ Otto e Mezzo, Da Vittorio, etc.) are 8–25 min by Line 2/10/1 from East Nanjing Road station. Proof: Transport → 1h isochrone.

  • Backup: Pudong Lujiazui (Grand Hyatt in Jin Mao Tower) — 12 min via Line 2 to Huangpu, river views, 15–20% cheaper on same nights in surrogate check; see Hotels ranking.
