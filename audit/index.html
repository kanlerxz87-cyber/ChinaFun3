Findings & next actions

IssueEvidence and next step
2024 described as latest guideThe legacy restaurant page calls 2024 the latest. Michelin’s 2026 announcement explicitly publishes a newer regional selection. Recheck each venue’s current Michelin page; regional totals are not Shanghai city totals.
Hotel eligibilityThe hotel comparison explicitly leaves most room-specific bed and bath details unverified. A star category does not establish the actual booked room’s amenities. Check each room type with its hotel and retain the official evidence.
Seven-night pricesThe hotel page reports November 25 snapshots and multiplies rounded starting nightly rates by seven. That is not necessarily the booking engine’s actual final seven-night total, and it may not match a qualifying room. Recheck dated, itemized quotes including taxes, occupancy and room category; compare all eligible date blocks before calling one cheapest.
Transit and rankingThe hotel table counts a partial set in the same district, not restaurants reached in an hour by transit. Measure door-to-door public transport routes for a consistent, complete Michelin set before ranking hotels on the requested criterion.
Social reviewsThe legacy restaurant table includes YouTube and TikTok search-result URLs. Searches and venue-owned social accounts are not verified individual reviews. Verify individual review links and flag missing ones.
Hours, prices, exceptionsExisting pages mix old menus, estimates and unverified fields. Check each venue’s own published hours, exceptions and menu for the travel dates; leave unavailable values blank and flagged. Compare the existing flags and official Michelin directory.
Scope of automated checksLocal tests passed for the considered and verify sites; they check structure, arithmetic and internal links, not whether each external source actually substantiates every cell, or whether every external URL works. The earlier verification record should not be read as a fresh audit of the whole repository.
