Suggestions for improving this site

A review queue, not a changelog. Nothing here has been implemented beyond what is marked "already implemented" — pick the path you want before any of it is built.

How to use this page. Each suggestion lists what it would add, why it changes a decision, and what it costs to build. Priority reflects decision impact, not effort. Approve in any order; the top three are the ones that would most change the answer to "which hotel, which nights".

Deliberately rejected

IdeaWhy not
Estimated hotel prices ("typical nightly rate")Would look verified and be wrong. The column stays empty and linked to the booking engine.
Auto-embedded social feedsEmbeds break silently and can't be verified offline; direct links age better.
Scraped aggregate ratingsThird-party scores change without notice and aren't official. Kept out entirely rather than half-trusted.
Filling hours from memory of similar venuesThe exact failure mode this project exists to avoid.

What would most improve the next iteration

  1. One verification session with the phone in hand — hours and set-menu prices for the twelve starred rooms you would realistically book. That single afternoon would fill more of this site's gaps than any amount of extra web reading.
  2. A dated price snapshot for the three shortlisted hotels, re-run monthly until booking.
  3. Routing confirmations for the top fifteen restaurants from the chosen hotel.
