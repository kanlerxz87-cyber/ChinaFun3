# Independent dashboard audit — 2026-11-26

Scope: sampled the root landing page, hotel and restaurant files, hotel and restaurant JSON, the link checker, test suites, and GitHub Pages. This is **not** a line-by-line verification of the whole repository. A successful structural test does not verify a factual claim or an external link.

## Findings requiring review

1. **Conflicting guide vintages.** The root landing page calls the collection “SHANGHAI 2024” while reporting 2026 counts; `hotels.html` describes counts from the 2024 list (51), while the [current MICHELIN starred directory](https://guide.michelin.com/en/cn/shanghai-municipality/shanghai/restaurants/all-starred) displays 50 (1 three-star, 11 two-star, 38 one-star). It does not establish any particular hotel's one-hour count. Recompute hotel ranks with a dated restaurant list and documented route results or leave counts and ranks unverified.
2. **Hotel pricing and booking timing are not established.** The landing page compares different hotels and surrogate historical dates to name March 1–8 the cheapest, and asserts a 45–75-day booking back-test. The inspected pages do not provide underlying dated observations sufficient to reproduce those claims. `data/hotels.json` labels a Grand Hyatt total `verified-proxy`, but links to a room-information page, not a reproducible dated checkout quote. Treat these claims and amounts as unverified until actual comparable quotes are captured with dates, room, occupancy, taxes and cancellation terms. Check directly with [Hyatt](https://www.hyatt.com/grand-hyatt/en-US/shagh-grand-hyatt-shanghai/rooms) and [Peninsula](https://www.peninsula.com/en/shanghai/rooms); neither link alone verifies the stated totals.
3. **Social links do not necessarily review the venue.** For example, the [Taian Table YouTube link](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Taian+Table+Shanghai) in `restaurants.html` is a search results page rather than a verified review. Search and hashtag links should be labeled as such, not presented as specific reviews; direct review links need individual checks.
4. **Source rule not demonstrated.** For example, the Da Vittorio row in `restaurants.html` cites a third-party 2024 list in its source cell, not an official Michelin or restaurant source for its stated current distinction and price. Recheck the row against the [official MICHELIN directory](https://guide.michelin.com/en/cn/shanghai-municipality/shanghai/restaurants/all-starred) and a current official menu; leave unconfirmed fields blank.
5. **Validation scope overstated.** `check_links.py` checks only nine `plan/*.html` pages and skips external URLs; it cannot establish that all pages or external links work. `verify` has a separate internal-link test. `npm test` passed eight tests for the considered site, and `verify` tests passed ten tests. These are structural checks, not a complete independent audit.
6. **Landing page assurances overstate coverage.** It says every restaurant and activity has days, hours and social videos, and all hotels are verified, while the above examples are search links and some price/routing evidence remains unverified. Replace categorical assurances with the actual coverage and link to the flags after row-level review.
