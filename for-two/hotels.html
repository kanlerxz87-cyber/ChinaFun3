How the eventual ranking should work

  1. Eligibility first: confirm two adults, king/queen-or-larger bed, private bathroom, actual availability and dated all-in rate.
  2. Count actual journeys: collect a full MICHELIN city inventory and deduplicate exact branches; test hotel-door to restaurant-door subway/bus routes, including walking, waits and transfers, at planned meal times.
  3. Rank on coverage: sort by the number of qualifying ≤60-minute venues. Show starred, Bib Gourmand and Selected counts separately. Break coverage ties with a lower comparable seven-night total.
  4. Compare like for like: breakfast, refundability, taxes, fees and room type must match before calling a rate cheaper. An unknown route or price is not zero.

This is a proposed method, not an implemented scoring system. No route has been certified in this session.
