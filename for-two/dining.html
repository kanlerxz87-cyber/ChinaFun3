Why these clusters?

Xintiandi pairs a starred Cantonese restaurant with a Bib Gourmand café. The Bund pairs one starred and one Selected restaurant at the same Peninsula address. Changning offers the three-star Taian Table and the two-star vegetarian Fu He Hui. Bao Li Xuan adds a two-star anchor in the Bvlgari complex by Suzhou Creek.

These are editorial address groupings, not verified one-hour travel zones. Each venue’s location and distinction is sourced in its row above.

Social media is inspiration only. Historic reviews can show an old menu, old star count or even the wrong branch. Missing social links are flagged; no search-result page is disguised as a venue video.
