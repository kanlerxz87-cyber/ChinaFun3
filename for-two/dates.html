When to book: what the evidence actually supports

Published offer window: 23 November–7 December 2026, for January–March 2027 stays. The engine says “Book before 8 Dec.” This is an evidenced promotion deadline, not proof that 7 December is the cheapest day to buy.

Rooms receive 15% off the applicable rate; the “up to 20%” headline concerns suites. This rate includes breakfast for up to two but requires full non-refundable prepayment and allows no amendments. Do not rush into it before entry eligibility, dates and budget are settled.

Planning suggestion: complete the decision checks before 7 December if considering this offer. A flexible rate may be safer when plans remain unresolved; its full conditions still require verification.
