THE CHINA EDIT / SOURCES & METHOD

Follow every finding to its source.

Official operator, government and MICHELIN evidence is kept separate from inspiration media. Checked on 25 November 2026.

What “verified” means here

Official facts

Hotel and restaurant operators support room, menu and rate facts. MICHELIN supports its own awards and published listings. Museum and government visitor pages support their stated schedules. The registry records the exact claim checked—not a blanket endorsement of every fact on a page.

Calculated, not invented

Hotel totals multiply a sourced nightly input by seven and apply an explicitly published surcharge. Two-person menu totals use sourced set prices or multiplication by two. Arithmetic is labeled; a calculated amount is not a final checkout quote.

Unknown stays unknown

Null means not confirmed. It is displayed as a blank value with a visible reason, never as zero, “free,” “closed,” or “sold out.” No travel duration is estimated. Current facts are not presented as guaranteed in 2027.

Media is separate

Social links are labeled as fetched, operator-linked or discovery-only. They are not used for official prices, awards or hours. Missing exact-branch matches stay flagged. Links may require login or stop working after this snapshot.
