Suggestions to discover the missing information

  1. Use the booking engine as the source of truth, not this site, for prices. Every hotel row links straight to it; the total quoted there is the only figure you should act on.
  2. Phone the restaurants. For starred Chinese dining rooms, hours and set-menu prices are routinely given by phone and rarely published. MICHELIN's contact number on each row is the fastest route.
  3. Record everything in one place as you confirm it — date checked, source, value. A verification timestamp is what turns a list into a booking plan.
  4. Re-check outdoor and museum venues within a week of travel: seasonal hours and holiday closures are the most volatile items in this dataset.
  5. Add a trip-file page to this site (see Suggestions) to hold confirmed values next to the research values, so the two never get confused.
