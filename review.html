Conflicting museum last-entry times

The current museum homepage states 15:00 for People’s Square during the Americas exhibition and requires advance tickets. An older museum notice states 16:00 and walk-in entry with peak-period exceptions. Use the current exhibition notice as a provisional reference, then confirm for 2027.

Michelin pages vary by edition

Different regional pages for Fu He Hui have shown different edition years and star counts. It is excluded from the shortlist until edition and current status are reconciled.

Schedules and price data are incomplete

Hours without a verified source, social videos, transit, room details and totals are explicitly blank. The small shortlist is not a complete guide inventory. No recommendation is a final booking decision.
