WORKING RECOMMENDATION

One base, less packing

Use Shanghai as a research starting point, with a seven-night baseline and optional longer stay. This is an editorial plan, not evidence that Shanghai is cheaper or denser than other Chinese cities.

BEFORE SPENDING

Keep the decision reversible

Do not make a non-refundable booking from this snapshot. A dated total, an eligible king room, refund conditions, and complete door-to-door routes are needed to support a hotel decision.
