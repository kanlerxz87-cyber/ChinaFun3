What the evidence can—and cannot—decide

Start with Verify for its source-first tables and explicit flags. The latest detailed root dashboard (re-verified Nov 26, 2026) is preserved below; it is a separate evidence snapshot, so compare each page’s own citations and flags rather than combining rankings.

  • Hotel fit: room-level queen/king bed and private-bathroom confirmation is incomplete. Do not assume every candidate meets both requirements; see Flag F5.
  • Cheapest dates: the stored rate snapshot covers Feb 15–22, 2027, checked Nov 25, 2026. Alternative blocks across Feb 15–Mar 23 are unverified, so no cheapest stay date or best booking day is established. See Hotels and Open Questions.
  • One-hour travel: the recorded research does not verify minute-level subway/bus routes. District proximity is not proof of a one-hour door-to-door trip; see Travel Time and Flag F4.
  • Dining totals: exact menu totals appear only where an official menu supports them. Other restaurant totals remain blank rather than estimated; see Michelin & Dining.
  • Michelin directory: the Verify snapshot records 50 starred entries in the 2026 live directory; its press release differs over 102 House. The discrepancy remains open rather than resolved by inference. See the official live directory and Flags F1–F2.
  • Hours and social links: coverage is partial. Each listed social link is recorded as venue-specific; a blank field means it was not confirmed in the research, not that no account exists.

Source and review standard

Use each category page’s source link to inspect the relevant official venue, government, Michelin, or booking-channel page. When a value could not be confirmed, the source-first pages leave it blank or label it unverified and route it to Flagged for Review. Social posts are not treated as proof of hours, prices, or Michelin status.

The /verify/ data records checks as of Nov 25, 2026; selected root corrections were logged Nov 26, 2026. Prices, availability, opening calendars, and restaurant distinctions can change. Re-open the official source before booking or visiting. No reservation or payment is made by this site.
