What to buy for transport

  • Metro ticket: Single-ride ¥3–¥6 (most rides in this plan). Or Shanghai Public Transport Card (20RMB deposit + top-up) works on Metro + bus. Buy at East Nanjing Rd station service center. shmetro.com fares
  • No “7-day pass” needed: Your weekly Metro spend for 2 is ~¥120–¥180 total (see Budget).
  • Bus backup: Lines 20, 37, 921 serve The Bund if Metro is crowded post-CNY. All accept transit card / Alipay transit QR.
